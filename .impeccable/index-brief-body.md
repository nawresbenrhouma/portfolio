## Direction contract

THESIS: A calm butter-and-terracotta page where the work sits on a white sheet in the first viewport: one featured engagement, told fully, with two more a tab away. It refuses the plain document look it replaces and the dark terminal template alike.

OWN-WORLD: Near-white ground with a touch of butter (#fffcf2) and a hero band in the same near-white (#fffcf2; the band is a layout zone, not a colour); pure white sheets (#ffffff) lifted by a soft offset shadow alone (0 12px 32px rgba(56,42,12,.14)); the 1px warm hairline (#e8dcb6) is reserved for rules inside sheets and for the dark theme, where a hairline replaces the shadow as the sheet's edge; espresso ink (#2b2417) and warm brown-grey body text (#5b4e35); one accent, terracotta (#a4441f for text, #c4532b for the button fill), on links, the Get-in-touch button, the selected folder tab and the current nav item; espresso as the secondary button. Dark theme: ground #1b170f, band #221c12, sheet #332c1e edged by a 1px #4a4130 hairline, cream text #f2e9d3, terracotta lifted to #eb8d63. Faces: Gabarito 700/800 for display and headings, tight (-0.03em) at display size; Source Sans 3 for text; Source Code Pro for technology names in chips. Radius 12px on sheets, 8px on controls, 6px on chips. 8px module; content max 76rem. Recognisable with content removed: a butter page, a white sheet with a tab row and a terracotta underline, heavy dark headline.

STORY: The visitor lands on the name and the Techem case study side by side and understands within seconds that this is a working backend engineer with a real production engagement. The tabs show breadth without leaving the fold. Scrolling gives the identity fields, a typed skills table on a sheet, a dated timeline, expandable project notes, learning, education and a contact sheet with the same three actions. Everything ends on "Get in touch".

FIRST VIEWPORT: Desktop 1440: header on the butter band, brand left, seven section anchors, "Get in touch" terracotta button and theme toggle right. Below, a two-column band: left 5 of 12: h1 "Nawres Ben Rhouma" in Gabarito 800 at clamp(2.25rem, 1.7rem + 2.2vw, 3rem), a step below the case title so the sheet owns the focal event, a 4rem terracotta rule, the positioning statement at 1.25rem, the availability line as a label/value row, the actions "View CV" (espresso) and LinkedIn / GitHub links with drawn SVG icons. Right 7 of 12: the "Selected work" sheet with folder tabs rising from its top edge (Techem WebPortal / Copilot Studio pilot / Consommi Tounsi), the selected tab fused into the sheet; inside: the case title at clamp(2.25rem, 1.6rem + 2.6vw, 3.5rem), the single focal event of the viewport, a subtitle, a role · employer · dates line, one paragraph, a "What I did" three-column breakdown with bold labels and notes of matched length, a stack row of code chips, a hairline, and a three-fact strip that sets one line per cell at 1440. Mobile 390: header brand + button + toggle, nav strip beneath; the text column, then the sheet full width with the tab row scrolling horizontally.

FORM: Reference-portfolio system (tinted page, white sheet, tabbed featured work, date-column timeline), brief-pinned by the user; no concept roll (a user-pinned direction beats the roll). Signature: folder tabs, the selected one fused into the sheet, a structure the reference does not have; the section nav underline slides as before (transform only, 220ms, cubic-bezier(0.2,0.8,0.2,1)); reduced motion swaps instantly. Tab ARIA is added by JavaScript; without it the tab row is hidden and the three panels stack as separate sheets. Below the fold the containers vary: About and Skills on sheets (About in two columns), Experience and Learning on the ground, Projects as expandable note sheets, Education on the ground, Contact on a two-column sheet (lead and actions | contact rows).
